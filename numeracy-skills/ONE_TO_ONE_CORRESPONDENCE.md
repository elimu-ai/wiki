# 👇🏽 One-to-one correspondence

[`NumeracySkill.ONE_TO_ONE_CORRESPONDENCE`](https://github.com/elimu-ai/model/blob/main/src/main/java/ai/elimu/model/v2/enums/content/NumeracySkill.java)

> Evaluates a child's ability to match one number word to one object during counting. Helps determine if the child understands _cardinality_ (that the final number said represents the total quantity of the set).
